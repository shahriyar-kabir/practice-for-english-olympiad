# Original Wizpiko word-puzzle collection

Ten newly authored themed crosswords replace the retired document-based collection. These are original writing and layouts for this release; this is not a legal clearance or a claim that common words are unique. All activities are untimed.

Historical saved answers remain under their retired puzzle IDs. They are not treated as solutions to these new activities.
